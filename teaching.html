Teaching Philosophy

My approach to teaching is centered on creating an inclusive, collaborative environment that encourages students to reach their full potential as learners. As a quantitative biologist my discipline is inherently collaborative; my research bridges the gap between statistics and biology to investigate applied conservation problems. Science thrives under diverse perspectives, and to this end I employ equitable, inclusive teaching and mentoring practices that build community around excitement for applied statistics.

Get Involved

I'm often looking for undergraduate students interested in ecology, applied statistical modeling, or data science. If you're interested in joining the lab or working on an independent project, reach out.
